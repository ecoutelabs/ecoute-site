# Écoute — product page ideas

Écoute is a focused, album-centric music player for macOS. Local files only.
Source lives at `ecoute/player/musicplayer`.

---

## What makes it distinct (copy hooks)

- **Album-first.** Most players treat music as a flat track list. Écoute is built around albums as the primary unit — browse by cover art, play front to back, stop at the end.
- **Local files, no cloud.** Your music, on your machine. No subscriptions, no accounts.
- **Last.fm scrobbling.** Plays well with the broader listening ecosystem.
- **Broad format support.** MP3, FLAC, ALAC, AAC, WAV, AIFF, OGG, Opus.
- **Fullscreen viewer.** Immersive playback with cover art front and center.
- **macOS native.** Built in Swift, feels like it belongs.

## Page structure ideas

### Option A — minimal, one-screen
- App name + tagline ("A music player for albums.")
- Single hero screenshot (mainview.png)
- Short bullet list of key features
- Download link (or "coming soon" if not publicly released yet)

### Option B — scrolling marketing page
- Hero with tagline + screenshot
- Feature sections (album browsing, format support, Last.fm, fullscreen)
- Screenshot or short demo video
- Download CTA at bottom

### Option C — just a landing with download
- Logo, one-liner, big download button
- Minimal — let the app speak for itself
